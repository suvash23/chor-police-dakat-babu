📖 Traditional Rules & Points

Roles & Traditional Values:

  • 👑 Babu (Saheb) - 1000 Pts: Master role. Always safe, commands Police.
  • 👮 Police - 800 Pts: Must identify who is the chosen suspect.
  • 🦹 Dakat - 600 Pts: Dangerous robber hiding in disguise.
  • 🦹 Chor - 0 Pts (or 400 Pts): Sneaky thief hiding in disguise.

Gameplay Flow:

  1. 4 folded chits are dealt to the 4 players.
  2. Babu and Police announce themselves automatically.
  3. Babu shouts orders: "Police, catch the Chor!" or "Police, catch the Dakat!"
  4. Police must pick between the 2 remaining hidden players.
  5. Scoring: If Police guesses correctly, everyone keeps their points. If Police guesses wrong, Police gets 0 pts and the target gets the 800 pts!
